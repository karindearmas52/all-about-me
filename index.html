Introducing Karin DeArmas

On my way towards journalism, I tripped and fell into a 30-year technology career. Now I'm back on that track in the Master of Arts in Engagement Journalism and also sub-specializing in Data and Audio. I knew Engagement and Data were going to be what I wanted to focus on, but I didn't see my interest in audio coming.

The articles below are three that I regularly re-read or reference for both inspiration in writing and in thinking and framing. Hope you enjoy them.

  1. Why AI writing is so generic, boring, and dangerous: Semantic ablation: This article is my response to anyone who talks about using AI for writing. When I first encountered it, I fed it into my Claude "Editorial Assistant" (which I forbid from writing) to critique it, and it predictably proceeded to do exactly what the piece tore apart. It's fleetingly satisfying when an AI chastises itself for obtuseness, but that was fun.
  2. Your Professional Decline Is Coming (Much) Sooner Than You Think: Do not let this headline fool or depress you! It's intentionally provocative, I believe, so that only the truly brave will venture past the doom and gloom of the headline. But make it through it and you'll end up transformed and inspired. I was.
  3. If you let AI do your writing, I will come to your house and kill you: Do not consume liquids during the reading of this piece. You will spew it on your screen in uproarious laughter. And by the end of it, you'll be ready to aid and abet murder for this cause.
